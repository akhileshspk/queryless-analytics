Jarvis AI

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with Jarvis AI via Google OAuth, we request access to specific Google Workspace APIs. We collect and process the following data strictly to provide AI-assisted workspace automation:

  • Gmail Data: Read-only access to summarize threads and send access to draft/send emails on your behalf.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Slides, & Sheets: Access to read spreadsheet data for analysis and write access to generate slide decks and document reports.
  • Google Calendar: Access to read upcoming events to detect scheduling conflicts and write access to create new calendar events.
  • Profile Information: Your email address to manage your subscription and isolated database routing.

2. How We Use Your Data (Google API Services User Data Policy)

Jarvis AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your workspace data is processed in real-time by our LLM pipeline (Gemma) to fulfill your direct chat prompts and scheduled background routines. We do not use your Google Workspace data to train generalized AI models, nor do we sell your data to third-party brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data, including OAuth tokens, chat histories, and scheduled routines, is stored in a strictly isolated, per-user SQLite database (`user_[id].db`). Only you have access to your generated chat history.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Razorpay to process subscription payments securely. Jarvis AI does not store your credit card information. Payments are subject to Razorpay's respective privacy policies.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may revoke Jarvis AI's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security settings. You may also cancel your subscription and request the deletion of your isolated database by contacting the developer.
